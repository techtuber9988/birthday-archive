Dear Darshika,

Happy Birthday to youuu! ♡

Sach bolun toh mujhe samajh nahi aa raha ki kahaan se start karun, because tere liye likhne ko kaafi kuch hai. Aur haan, ye mat sochna ki main suddenly itna accha ho gaya hoon. Main abhi bhi tujhe irritate karunga. That's a lifetime offer.

Jab hum second semester mein friends bane the, tab honestly mujhe nahi pata tha ki tu meri life ka itna important part ban jayegi. Dheere-dheere assignments, college ki bakchodi, random talks, gossip, late-night conversations aur pata nahi kitni chhoti-chhoti cheezein humari friendship ka part ban gayi.

Kabhi main tere assignments kar raha hota hoon, kabhi tu mujhe padhne mein help karti hai, kabhi hum apni problems discuss karte hain, aur kabhi bina kisi reason ke ek dusre ko pareshan karte rehte hain. Aur somehow, ye sab normal cheezein hi itni special ban gayi hain.

Mujhe teri ek baat genuinely achhi lagti hai — tu apne tareeke se care karti hai. Kabhi daant ke, kabhi samjha ke, kabhi meri bakwaas sun ke, aur kabhi mujhe meri hi harkaton ke liye suna ke. Especially jab tune smoking ko lekar mujhe daanta tha, tab mujhe samajh aaya ki meri fikr karne wale log bhi hain jo mujhe galat direction mein jaate nahi dekhna chahte. I might joke about it, but I do value that.

Aur ek baat — teri friendship ne mujhe ye bhi feel karaya ki har friendship same nahi hoti. Kabhi-kabhi past experiences ki wajah se trust karna difficult ho jaata hai, but tere saath maine phir se samjha ki ek genuine friendship kitni comforting ho sakti hai. Thank you for being that person, without even making a big deal out of it.

I hope tu hamesha aise hi rahe — thodi pagal, thodi dramatic, thodi overthinking queen, thodi bossy, but dil se achhi. I hope tujhe life mein woh sab mile jo tu deserve karti hai: happiness, peace, success, genuine people, aur aise moments jahan tu bina kisi tension ke bas khush reh sake.

Jab bhi life overwhelming lage, ya ghar, college, ya kisi bhi cheez ki wajah se mood off ho, I hope tujhe yaad rahe ki tujhe sab kuch akele handle karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Main har problem solve kar paun, zaroori nahi — but main teri baat sun sakta hoon, tere saath has sakta hoon, aur zaroorat padne par tujhe reality check bhi de sakta hoon. Haan, free mein. Lucky you. 😂

Thank you for the late-night talks, the trust, the food, the laughs, the random stories, the daant, and all those little moments jo shayad kisi aur ko ordinary lagenge, but mere liye memories hain. Thank you for being my friend in your own, completely unpredictable way.

Aur madam, Domino's wala promise yaad hai na? Birthday wishes accept karne se pehle pizza ka pending bill clear karna zaroori nahi hai, but reminder toh banta hai. 🍕

I don't know ki aage college ke baad life kitni busy ho jaayegi, hum kitne change honge, ya kitni cheezein different ho jaayengi. But I genuinely hope humari friendship mein hamesha woh comfort rahe — jahan hum bina pretend kiye apne real selves ho sakein.

You deserve to be loved, respected, appreciated, and celebrated not just on your birthday, but on the random ordinary days too. Never forget how much you matter, okay?

Happy Birthday once again, pagal aurattt. ♥

Here's to more college memories, more random conversations, more food plans that actually happen, more bakchodi, and more years of this beautiful friendship.

With endless sab kuch (love , care , friendship and thodi irritation),

Yours Param Mitra, Ayush ♡
